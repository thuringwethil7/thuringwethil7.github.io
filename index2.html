About

Joanne Wheeler is a flute player and tour guide from the Boston area. She is a versatile performer, bringing audiences music from a wide range of genres and time periods. Equipped with a flute and a speaker, she fills nursing homes, assisted living centers, and parks with live, engaging music that gets people dancing in their seats.

PROGRAMS

Programs can be customized for your residents - get in touch to learn more! Programs from the past year have included holidays like Christmas and Hanukkah, Valentine's Day, St. Patrick's Day, and the 4th of July; a Showcase featuring hits from the 50s and 60s, classical standards, and more!
